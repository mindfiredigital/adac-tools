First publication.

- Validates ADAC 0.1 and 0.2 files.
- Supports `layoutOptions`, `layoutOptions.defaults` (`v1` or `none`), `visual.layout`, `visual.style` and connection `layout` and `bidirectional`.
- CommonJS and ES module builds with TypeScript types.
- Requires Node 20 or newer.
